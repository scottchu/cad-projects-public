## Story spine

I bought a printer and started with downloaded models. Wanting to make my own ideas exposed a stack of unfamiliar skills. The agent became both an assistant and a teacher. Real projects then exposed what neither a plausible model nor a finished print could settle. We accumulated useful fit knowledge, built focused interfaces to understand constraints, and connected tools to reduce execution friction. The projects and the way we worked developed together. Laifen shows what that approach looks like now.

Keep the current title, light engineering style, actual artifacts and personal show-and-tell voice. The feedback lesson should emerge from the work. No invented dates, dialogue, emotional details, print counts, successful outcomes or claims that every revision was printed. The proposed sequence has 20 main slides and can retain the existing 25-minute pacing envelope, including the 90-second Laifen demo.
