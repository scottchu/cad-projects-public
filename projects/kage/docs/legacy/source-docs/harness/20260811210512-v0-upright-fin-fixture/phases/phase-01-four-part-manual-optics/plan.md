# Phase 01 — four-part manual optics

Intent: prove manual concentric rotation and upright-fin shadow geometry before introducing a real quartz movement or final light housing.

Parts:
1. Fake quartz/mask body with integrated stepped arbor.
2. Hour carrier-hand.
3. Minute carrier-hand.
4. Second carrier-hand.

Frozen optical contract:
- LED source reference: 60 mm from wall.
- Carrier radii H/M/S: 30.00 / 34.75 / 39.50 mm.
- Shadow intervals: 48→85 / 48→110 / 48→130 mm.
- Upright-fin working Z: H 22.500→38.823529; M 16.562500→41.045455; S 10.625000→41.769231 mm.

Mechanical contract:
- Support-free for 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers.
- Exactly four connected positive-volume printed solids.
- Lower carriers rotate on 0.30 mm diametral-clearance journals.
- Removable split-collet hour hub is the sole axial retainer.
- No traditional radial shadow blade, X, star, or counter-fin.

Verification:
- Formula, fits, bounds, clearances, axial planes, collision sweeps, solid connectivity, mesh manifoldness, four-object 3MF, source/export agreement.
- Mandatory multi-view snapshots and independent evaluator verdict.
- Physical force, friction, wear, runout, and real shadow quality remain print-test gates.
