# Phase 01 proof contract

## Claim

At least two mechanically distinct, understandable low-Z concepts can be derived from the locked direct-gear-race geometry while preserving the ring and fin interfaces.

## Output contract

- Locked-baseline measurement table.
- Two or three concept definitions with named parameters and explicit Z budgets.
- One recommended concept for the next physical mechanism prototype.
- Individual STEP concepts plus one side-by-side comparison STEP in a new experimental namespace.

## TDD applicability

Required for generated geometry.

## Required red evidence

Before implementation, focused tests must fail because the experimental module and STEP outputs do not exist. The tests must also encode the locked baseline's measured 56.7514 mm complete envelope and immutable source digest.

## Green evidence

Focused tests prove each exported concept's actual STEP Z envelope, positive solids, retained ring/pinion/fin datums, and absence of unintended collisions in its intended static pose.

## Refactor/regression evidence

Rerun the focused suite after final exports and rerun the locked baseline tests/hash check.

## Evidence capture requirements

Required: fresh STEP imports, measured bounding boxes, and rendered snapshots of every individual concept and the side-by-side comparison.

## Route evidence gate

Evaluator may ship the exploration only if locked files are unchanged, every concept is clearly derived from locked interfaces, actual STEP measurements show a meaningful mechanism-depth reduction, and snapshots make the mechanism understandable. Physical motion, noise, torque, wear, and print fit remain deferred.

## Required feedback loop

Independent CAD evaluator reviews the generated artifacts after the generator finishes.

## Supporting feedback loops

Actual-solid collision checks and visual snapshot review.

## Deferred feedback loops

Physical printing, motor torque/noise, friction, bearing wear, thermal behavior, and long-duration timekeeping.
