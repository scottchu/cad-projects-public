# V0 upright-fin manual fixture — physical test plan

This four-part fixture proves manual rotation and shadow geometry only. It is
not compatible with a real quartz movement.

Print all four objects at 0.20 mm layers with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Keep their
provided flat orientations and do not scale them.

1. Inspect the Ø12/8/6/4 stepped arbor and remove only loose stringing.
2. Install second, then minute, then hour. The lower two should rotate freely;
   the split-collet hour hub is the sole axial retainer.
3. Rotate every carrier through 360°. Fail for binding, lifting, spoke/fin
   contact, cracks, or a carrier escaping the stack.
4. Place a current-limited point LED at the 60 mm reference distance. Manually
   rotate each carrier and check that each upright rim fin casts a distinct
   radial shadow. The expected wall radii are 48→85, 48→110, and 48→130 mm.
5. Record rotational force, wobble/runout, collet whitening, shadow sharpness,
   and whether the mask hides all non-fin geometry.

CAD does not prove force, friction, wear, runout, heat safety, or real shadow
quality. Those remain physical gates before designing the real clock.
