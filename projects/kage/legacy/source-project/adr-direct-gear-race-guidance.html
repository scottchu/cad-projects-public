CONTEXT

The prior concept added geometry solely for guidance.

The user intent is simpler: the existing carrier must do double duty as gear body and running race. The tooth face remains a separate non-contact drive envelope.

DECISION

Run relieved rollers on the smooth lower carrier root land.

Three towers sit at 60°, 180°, and 300°. The running land reaches R131.55, leaving 0.05 mm nominal gap to the R131.60 carrier cylinder. Flanges provide 0.80 mm radial capture.

CONSEQUENCES

Fewer parts and no added guidance feature.

The same carrier body now supplies rotation, drive teeth, and a lower contact land. Accurate axial relief and tooth clearance become critical, especially at the adjustable tower.

TESTS

Carrier-only contact is necessary but not physical proof.

CAD must verify tooth clearance, axial relief, and free rotation. Printed seam bump, friction, wear, noise, and adjustment remain physical test gates.
