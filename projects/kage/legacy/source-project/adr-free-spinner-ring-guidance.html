CONTEXT

The existing keepers consume the entire under-stack service zone.

Rails, shoes, caps, and spines obscure the rings and create long printed rub surfaces. The three carriers still need radial and axial capture without loading the internal drive teeth.

DECISION

Use three towers with one deep-grooved free spinner per ring.

Towers sit at 60°, 180°, and 300°. The third tower adjusts ±0.25 mm; the other two establish the datum. Every roller turns independently on the carrier's compact integral bead.

CONSEQUENCES

Cleaner service access, but nine wear interfaces must be proven.

The architecture removes the continuous keeper rail and separates drive from guidance. It adds small rollers, pins, preload setup, and material choices that CAD cannot settle.

TESTS

Print fit, friction, wear, noise, and preload remain physical gates.
