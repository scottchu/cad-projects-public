# KAGE V3 isolated drive-service station

This packet proves one canonical minute-channel drive station. It does not
modify or replace the existing three-ring mechanism.

## Hardware

- 1 x NEMA8 stepper, 20.3 mm face, 15.4 mm mounting pitch, 4 mm x 7 mm D shaft
- 4 x M2x4 socket-head screws for the motor face
- 4 x M3x8 screws for the removable motor cartridge and gearbox-frame feet
- 2 x M3x8 screws plus two M3 heat-set inserts for the upper bearing bridge
- 2 x MR84ZZ bearings, 4 x 8 x 3 mm
- 1 x 4 x 40 mm steel D shaft, 3.5 mm across-flat
- 2 x commercial 4 mm split shaft collars
- 2 x M1 16T straight-miter gears, 1:1
- 1 x existing M1 16T D-bored output pinion

## Assembly order

1. Bolt the NEMA8 to the removable motor cartridge with four M2x4 screws.
2. Clamp the horizontal miter gear onto the motor D shaft.
3. Bolt the motor cartridge and the separate two-sided gearbox frame to the
   chassis using their M3x8 screws.
4. Seat the lower MR84. Fit the lower split collar and lower inner-race spacer
   to the output shaft, then insert the shaft upward while placing the vertical
   miter gear between the two side supports.
5. Seat the upper MR84 and fasten the removable upper bridge with two M3x8
   screws into inserts.
6. Add the minute height spacer, clamp the output pinion at Z=4.85..6.95 mm,
   then install the upper safety collar and its removable printed hand grip.

The printed grip is not a structural plastic thread. The commercial split
collar supplies retention on the smooth steel D shaft.
