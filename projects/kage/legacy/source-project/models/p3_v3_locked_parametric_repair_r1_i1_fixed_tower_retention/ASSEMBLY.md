# Phase-2A I1 fixed-tower retention

This packet changes only the fixed roller towers at 60 and 180 degrees. The
accepted locked clock and Phase-1 artifacts remain immutable baselines.

## Hardware per fixed tower

- one D3.99 x 30 mm stock axle, trimmed by 0.5486 mm to a 29.4514 mm installed span;
- one D8 / D4 x 0.15 mm washer;
- one intact D8 / D4 x 4 mm set-screw collar;
- one ISO 4026 M3 x 2 flat-point set screw with 1.5 mm internal hex.

## Required post-print operation

Tap the printed D2.50 radial pilot M3 x 0.5 before installing the set screw.
The D2.50 pilot is not a finished M3 thread. Do not force the screw into the
untapped print.

## Assembly

1. Trim the stock axle to 29.4514 mm and deburr both ends.
2. Insert the axle into the blind D4.05 x 3.00 mm lower seat and through the
   D4.06 upper bridge.
3. Add the D8 / D4 x 0.15 mm washer.
4. Slide the intact collar onto the axle, leaving the validated 0.10 mm axial
   endplay.
5. Install the tapped ISO 4026 M3 x 2 set screw. Its nominal outer hex face is
   0.005 mm below the collar OD and its flat tip is tangent to the D3.99 axle.
6. The screw has a documented +/-0.20 mm adjustment allowance. Tightening
   preload, printed-thread strength, and holding torque remain physical-test
   items; CAD proves only geometry and access.

The complete axle and D6.50 service path were checked at 41 withdrawal states.
This is CAD acceptance, not proof of printed fit, wear, or clamp force.
