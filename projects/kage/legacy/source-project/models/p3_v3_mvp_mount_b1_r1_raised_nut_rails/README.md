# B1-R1 raised captive-nut rail MVP

This isolated revision proves only the hard mechanical mounting architecture on
the immutable Q4-R4 four-quadrant base. It contains exactly seven reusable
printed objects: four localized quadrants, one motor sled, one fixed riser, and
one spine riser. Motors, gears, bearings, rings, fins, and electronics are not
part of this MVP.

The model uses top-only M3x8 fasteners with rigid AF5.5 x 2.4 mm captive nuts in
raised, open-ended receivers. Motor nuts are clocked -30 degrees so AF5.5 spans
the 5.8 mm channel, giving 0.15 mm clearance per side. A 7 mm localized approach
trench proves rigid insertion without relying on snap deformation. The three
motor stations provide +/-2.5 mm radial sled travel. Fixed and spine risers are
cut after fusion with D3.4 shaft paths and continuous D6.5 driver chimneys. The
installed fixed riser at 60 degrees and spine riser at 300 degrees terminate at
Z=-5.2 mm.

CAD validation cannot prove printed nut loading/removal, adjustment-cycle wear,
10 N clamp slip/yaw, or sled/riser stiffness. Those are mandatory physical tests
before any motor or transmission geometry is authorized.
