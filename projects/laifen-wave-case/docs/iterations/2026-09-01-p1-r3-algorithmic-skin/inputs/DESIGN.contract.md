## Continuous faceted diamond skin

Choose shared integer N=`floor(2*pi*Rc/skin_arc_pitch_target+0.5)`; default31, shared by body and cap. Exact closure uses N, not a rounded angular step. Local developed arc pitches are Pb=2*pi*Rb/N=3.526691 and Pc=2*pi*Rc/N=3.628033; different radii keep the SAME N and phase.

Let u=N*(theta-theta0)/(2*pi), v=(z-Z0)/pz; w(t)=t-floor(t+0.5) in[-0.5,0.5). Set m=max(abs(w(u+v)),abs(w(u-v))) and Fd=min(1,max(0,(0.5-m)/(0.5-a))). This is a periodic field of truncated diamond pyramids: adjacent bases meet along shared zero-width valley lines, with no smooth cylindrical gaps or detached solids between cells. Every open area of the band belongs to a face or blunt plateau. It is an original full tiling, not copies of the reference geometry or R2 frusta.

For a band [lo,hi], let d=min(z-lo,hi-z). Envelope e=0 outside; e=0.5*(1-cos(pi*d/f)) for0<d<f; e=1 for d>=f. The exact radial surface is `r(theta,z)=R+h*e(z)*Fd(u,v)`. It meets the unchanged cylindrical shell continuously with zero axial derivative at band ends. Bands include two2mm fades, leaving20mm of full-amplitude skin. Functional smooth clearances are OUTSIDE the band; fade zones do not count toward the required2mm exclusions.

Period is pz independent of length; count n=floor((hi-lo)/pz), default5 whole periods, with any remainder naturally clipped by the fixed envelope. Lattice center rows occur at v=j/2 and u=i+(j mod2)/2, so pitch4.8 gives nominal2.4mm alternating center-row spacing. Evaluate the global field, including partial cells at band boundaries; do not resize cells or leave an unused tail because a complete row does not fit. theta=0 and2pi and their one-sided derivatives match by integer periodicity. Nominal assembled shared phase is explicit; physical tightening-angle registration remains unproven.

The developed plateau is a diamond with half-diagonals a*P and a*pz. Its minimum width is `2*a*P*pz/sqrt(P^2+pz^2)`: body about1.023, cap about1.043. Crest is a finite blunt terrace, not an apex. On the exact radial surface the terrace follows a cylinder; it is not claimed to be one planar tangent face. Macro flanks are piecewise linear in the developed coordinates and curved only by cylinder wrapping. This avoids reusing R2's inaccurate visual assumption that sparse planar solids equal a skin.

Support budget: |dFd/dz|<=1/(pz*(0.5-a)); |de/dz|<=pi/(2f). Therefore |dr/dz|<=h*(1/(pz*(0.5-a))+pi/(2f))=about0.6464, below1. For a radial graph, true normal downward ratio is |r_z|/sqrt(1+(r_theta/r)^2)<=|r_z|; this conservative bound is about32.9deg from vertical in either upright direction. Slope edges use one-sided normals. Actual CAD tessellation/fitting must preserve the bound and be checked, not inferred from this equation alone.

Optional comparison `Fs=1-sin(pi*(u+v))^2*sin(pi*(u-v))^2` is a connected smooth crossed-ridge skin using the same datums/envelope. Its conservative slope bound h*(2*pi/pz+pi/(2f))=0.94248 (<45deg). It changes tactile intent; user selection is required to replace the default faceted diamonds.
