## Answer

They share the broad product architecture and tactile direction: a coaxial,
two-piece cylindrical case, with a male-threaded lower part, a female-threaded
cap, a shoulder-stop closure, and repeated diamond-like exterior relief on both
parts. They do **not** share the same demonstrated design method. The download
is only a triangulated mesh; it shows geometry, not its author's parameters,
generator, clearances, or thread equations. R4 is deliberately specified as a
parameterized, continuous, common-phase diamond field and preserves the known
R3 p2/E6/c25 functional thread construction.
