## Original drain/vent pattern

Each floor has seven axial through-holes diameter3: one at(0,0), six at(9*cos(k*pi/3),9*sin(k*pi/3)), k=0..5. This original simple polar construction supports drainage/venting from either end; the reference's seven holes inform function only. All hole tools cross only the respective3mm floor and stop just inside the cavity; they must not cut threads, stop annulus or cylindrical sidewall. Hole edges are straight, without top countersinks, hidden roofs or chamfers; printing openings-up starts every hole at the bed and leaves vertical hole walls. Recessed R4 center C25 digits are omitted because they conflict with the center opening; retain the C25 identity in source names/artifact labels. Nearest hole center spacing9; nominal hole-to-hole ligament6. Hole outermost radius10.5 leaves4.5 to body bore15 and5.0 to cap bore15.5; closed-floor perimeter remains connected. Seven-hole area7*pi*1.5^2=49.4801mm2 per end. Holes alone do not promise complete drying: flat-table or handle-bottom contact can occlude outlets. The bounded test is water passage through unobstructed/tilted empty halves. Full-case end-contact policy determines whether channels/stand-offs or device supports are necessary; that is not silently solved here. No sidewall holes or unsupported horizontal ceilings are introduced.
