# OpenSCAD Customizer V1 design contract

All units are mm. `device_length L=208`, `axial_allowance A=5`, and `cavity C=L+A=213`. Closed length is `body_height + cap_height - overlap = 144+84-9=219`; the 9 mm closure overlap is derived from the existing thread/shoulder contract. Split distance `S` is measured from the top and is canonical; percentage is a projection `100*S/H`, never a second source. If percentage mode is selected, convert once to `S=H*p/100`, then derive all geometry. `body_bore=30`, `cap_bore=31`, and `wall=2.4` yield outer radius from OD and independent radial readouts; device diameter is reference-only.

Thread engagement is `E=pitch*turns` (default `2*3=6`), with clearance/side in `[0.20,0.30]`, depth/height `0.5`, and lead/runout excluded from effective engagement. Chamfer is `1.2` with domain `0.8..1.5` and below floor. Diamond repeat count is the nearest integral circumferential count from arc pitch, with phase `theta=2*pi*k/count + phase0` and axial phase `z mod axial_pitch`; body and cap share the same phase at the seam. Drains are seven holes: centre plus six at ring radius9, selected by drainage mode.

Assert finite positive dimensions; `C >= 2*floor + E + 3`; OD exceeds each bore plus twice wall; chamfer is positive and less than floor; pitch, turns, and clearance are valid; split lies strictly inside the feasible body/cap interval; drain cutters remain inside the floor; and print-layout objects have positive spacing and no intersection. The body owns the external positive shoulder; the cap closes against it while the device never acts as a stop. Body and cap remain separate solids in `body`, `cap`, and `print_layout`, and are joined only in `assembled`.

Default orientation is openings-up, supports off, with exposed slopes at or below45 degrees and smooth zones at thread, shoulder, floor, and opening transitions. The implementation must provide a pure parameter/derivation layer, one geometry source, named output selectors, deterministic round-trip tests for split projection, endpoint bounds, phase continuity, and output selection. The CLI must render each selected output to STL, validate manifold/solid counts and bounds, and write hash-addressed reports. Required snapshots cover assembled, body, cap, print layout, section/clearance, and drain views at default and one changed/reset parameter state. No implementation, STL, slicing, printer action, or physical acceptance belongs in this iteration.
