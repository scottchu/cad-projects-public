# P18 R1 brief

P18 R1 is the compact 45-degree 3D product-design baseline explicitly
requested after approval of the P17 2D optimizer candidate. It preserves the
P17 four-plate, three-rod, fixed-inner/opposite-outer-twist architecture while
changing the product envelope, fixed-axle placement and total twist travel.

P17 remains historical evidence. P18 must be implemented in separate source
and artifact paths and must not be described as physically proven before a
print and assembly trial.
