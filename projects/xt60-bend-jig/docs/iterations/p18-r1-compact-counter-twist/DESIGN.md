# P18 R1 frozen 3D design contract

Design provenance: `/root/three_rod_design` completed the mechanism analysis;
`/root/p17_optimizer_math_audit` independently audited the numeric mapping.
This file freezes their joined contract for implementation.

## Geometry and state law

- `theta_end = 45 deg`, `alpha_left(u)=-45u`,
  `alpha_right(u)=+45u`, `u in [0,1]`.
- `C=(0,0) mm`, `A=(-9.5,-9.5) mm`, and the accepted common rigid placement
  is `S=(-14.6869657526,-12.2949747468) mm` in FRONT YZ.
- Rod paths are the exact current P17 `GUIDES + S`.
- XT60 body, wire, bend, solder and service references are the P17 source
  references plus `D13_COMMON_ROD_DELTA + S`. This aligns the stale source
  workpiece references with the already-shifted P17 guide table; it is not an
  independent rod/workpiece translation.
- Common rod travel is `10.5852813742 mm`; geometric preload remains
  `0.25 mm`; `u_contact=0.976382299991634`; therefore
  `theta_contact=43.9372034996235 deg` and END is exactly `45 deg`.
- OPEN and END rod coordinates, workpiece orientation, identities and contact
  roles remain exactly those frozen by the accepted P17 optimizer candidate.

## Plate and rail law

- Inner plate `OD52.933`; left outer `OD53.933`; right force outer `OD54.933`.
- Inner plate and both outer plate blanks are centered on virtual datum `C`.
  There is no centered pivot bore or new physical member at `C`.
- Fixed-inner tracks are straight capsule sweeps of the rod center paths.
- Each outer rod rail is generated by the deterministic inverse transform
  `q_s(u)=R(-alpha_s(u))*(p_rod(u)-C)`.
- Each outer fixed-axle rail is generated by
  `qA_s(u)=R(-alpha_s(u))*(A-C)`.
- Rail sample authority is at least 33 odd samples; validation uses a denser
  independent sample set and continuous/chord-error bounds.
- Slot widths remain `4.15 mm` for `OD4.00 mm` rods. Axle-guide width remains
  `7.90 mm` for the `OD7.60 mm` journal.
- Required actual-material web is `>=2.20 mm`. The 2D selected internal and
  rounded-boundary values are evidence inputs only; exact BREP distance and
  written STEP parity control 3D acceptance.

## Axial architecture

- Preserve P17 axial stack: inner clear span `18.40 mm`; inner and outer
  plate thicknesses `3.20 mm`; inner/outer running gap `0.40 mm`; principal
  stack `32.00 mm`; cap/outer gap `0.30 mm`; cap engagement `8.70 mm`; rod
  length exactly `50.00 mm`.
- Preserve the one-piece stepped axle: AF10 central spacer, AF8.00 keys,
  actual AF8.15 fixed-inner bores, OD7.60 round journals, OD7.90 outer running
  guide/bore, retained OD10/bore7.55 keepers. Axle total length is `34.90 mm`
  and each side beyond the central spacer is `8.25 mm`.
- Model and measure the regular hex exactly. AF8.15 circumradius is
  `4.705404693895 mm`; `AF/2` is forbidden as the 3D clearance proxy.
- The axle and inner plates are one world-fixed keyed group. Outer plates
  rotate independently on the round journal/guide network.

## Mandatory evidence

1. Math: inverse reconstruction error for all rods and A on both sides;
   correct OPEN/contact/END state law; unchanged triangles and identities.
2. Constraint: full four-contact Jacobian at dense samples, rank `3` and a
   finite recorded normalized condition. The design probe found rank `3` and
   worst normalized condition about `3.7503`; validation must reproduce it.
   A diagonal rail becomes nearly tangent near `u≈0.969` and has only about
   `0.34065/0.35900 deg` crossing at contact; record this as a physical
   force/friction risk, not a digital failure by itself.
3. Actual solids: zero unintended plate/rod/cap/axle/keeper/workpiece
   intersections across dense motion; exact BREP web `>=2.20 mm`; single-solid
   printable parts; no unsupported floating keeper or axle segment.
4. Service: actual-solid XT60, wire, solder access, cooling and completed-
   pigtail removal sweep with at least the existing `0.75 mm` clearance gate.
5. Artifact: explicit OPEN/contact/END/exploded/print-kit/full-motion STEP
   generation, STEP validation, refs census and source/import bound parity.
6. Visual: deterministic FRONT, opposite, isometric and exploded snapshots;
   stationary holes/rails relative to their owning plates; visible opposite
   outer twist; correct head/up and wires/right orientation.

## Smallest repair policy

Implementation may repair only source-level stale constants, labels,
state-table calculations, derived placement and exact-geometry bugs needed to
materialize this contract. Any change to rod coordinates, A, C, S, plate ODs,
45-degree travel, topology, workpiece geometry, axial architecture, part count
or minimum web reopens design and requires a new 2D gate. No force, retention,
lock, self-lock, fit, heat, wear or physical acceptance claim is authorized.
