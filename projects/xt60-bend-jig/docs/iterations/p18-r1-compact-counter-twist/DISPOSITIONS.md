# P18 R1 critique dispositions

This joins the immutable critique and validation receipts without changing the
candidate fingerprint.

- **C1 near-tangent rail:** digitally dispositioned by independent dense
  branch, derivative, four-contact rank/condition, actual-BREP and full
  two-sided support gates. The candidate remains valid as prescribed CAD
  motion. Clearance-amplified hand force, friction, wedging, backlash and
  back-drive are explicitly **not accepted** and remain a first physical
  assembly test gate.
- **C2 solder-tool access:** not closed as a full-assembly soldering-iron solid
  sweep. The pigtail/body/wire removal path and axle/keeper keepout checks pass,
  but P18 makes no claim that a real iron clears every plate, rod and cap.
  Solder-tool access remains an explicit physical/service validation gap and
  must not be described as CAD-accepted.
- **C3 virtual datum C:** source ownership and snapshots confirm there is no
  authored centered pivot bore or physical member. The diagnostic boolean is
  false only because a functional derived rail crosses C; it is not evidence
  of a hidden pivot. No candidate change is required.
- **C4-C7:** cap/keeper permanence, tolerance robustness at the limiting web,
  perceptible right-side force advantage, axle print orientation and all PETG
  fit/heat/wear behavior remain physical gates. No digital or physical proof is
  claimed.

Formal review may accept the frozen CAD/STEP candidate only with the above
acceptance boundary. Slicing, printing, full solder-tool access, acceptable
hand torque and physical acceptance remain outside this candidate gate.
