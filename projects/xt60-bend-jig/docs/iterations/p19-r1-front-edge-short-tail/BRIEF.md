# P19 R1 front-edge short-tail brief

P18 proved a compact 45-degree counter-twist mechanism but did not validate a
real soldering-iron approach to short FPV ESC leads. P19 retains the four-plate,
three-rod counter-twist architecture while changing the working-area placement
and rail motion law so the connector tail and solder joint sit at or beyond the
right/front plate edge.

The first acceptance gate is 2D only. No 3D implementation is authorized until
the service corridor, OPEN/END topology, rail sweeps, plate boundary, and fixed
axle position are visible together and the user confirms the direction.
