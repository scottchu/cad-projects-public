# P6 two-plate rod coupon: physical test boundary

P6 is a minimal fit and plate-thickness test. It is not a complete bending
mechanism and it is not self-fixturing.

## Assembly order

1. Press the measured 4 mm rod through the nominal 3.90 mm fixed plate.
2. Seat the nominal 3.90 mm fixed blind cap on that end.
3. Slide the nominal 4.05 mm service plate onto the rod.
4. Put the 38 mm gauge between the two inner plate faces, below and off-axis
   from the rod. Do not center the solid gauge on the rod.
5. Rest both square plate edges on the same flat bench datum, then clamp each
   plate externally. The gauge sets spacing; the datum and two clamps set
   parallelism and angle.
6. Seat the nominal 3.95 mm removable service cap.
7. Remove the gauge sideways before soldering.

The caps stop the plates leaving the rod outward. They do not stop the loose
plate moving inward. Do not rely on either cap to preserve the 38 mm gap.

## Print tests

- Print the 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, and 3.5 mm square plates flat on a large face.
- The keyed hole ladder reads from its notched end: 3.85, 3.90, 3.95, 4.00,
  4.05, then 4.10 mm.
- The four cap-fit coupons are 3.85, 3.90, 3.95, and 4.00 mm.
- All coupon bore axes are vertical in the supplied print layouts so the
  diameter comparison uses the same print orientation.

Record actual rod diameter, filament, nozzle, layer height, wall count, plate
flex under clamp load, cap pull-off behavior, and whether the service plate
creeps inward under gravity. CAD validity does not prove printed fit, strength,
heat resistance, or safe soldering clearance.
