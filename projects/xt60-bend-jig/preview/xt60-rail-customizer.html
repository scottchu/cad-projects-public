How the rails are calculated

The fixed inner track joins each OPEN and END point with a straight line. At progress u, the rod is at P(u). A rotating outer plate needs the local slot R(−θu)P(u). Rotating that slot point forward by θu returns the same rod position.

The purple axle stays fixed in the world. Its outer guide is the inverse rotation of the profile’s fixed axle position, (−10, −10) by default. The inner purple circle is an axle reference, not its hex-bore cut.
